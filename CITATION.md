# How to cite a paper card

## Chinese

> 曹溪能（Xixi Cao），《[论文卡片中文标题]》，physics+AI 每日论文卡，
> [卡片发布日期]，[卡片永久链接]（访问日期：YYYY-MM-DD）。

## English

> Cao, Xineng (Xixi Cao). "[Paper card title]." *physics+AI Daily Paper
> Cards*, [publication date]. [permanent card URL] (accessed YYYY-MM-DD).

Also cite the underlying paper using its authors, title, venue or arXiv record,
year, and DOI/arXiv ID. The paper-card citation acknowledges the independent
Chinese synthesis; it does not replace the original paper citation.

When adapting a card, state that it was adapted, describe material changes,
and retain the CC BY-NC 4.0 link. Commercial reuse requires separate written
permission.

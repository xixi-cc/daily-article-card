TITLE · ABSTRACT

Banded non-Hermitian random matrices, neural networks, and eigenvalue degeneracies

带状非厄米随机矩阵、神经网络与本征值简并

We study two-banded, non-Hermitian random matrices inspired by sparse neural networks with a circular, 1d topology. We focus on two paradigmatic models, an SSH chain and a ladder model, which have both non-Hermitian directional bias and random sign disorder in the hoppings. The random sign disorder, which follows Dale's Law, leads to localization of the eigenstates, while the directional bias drives a delocalization transition in these states. The competition between disorder and directional bias results in rich eigenspectra with loops of extended states in the complex plane surrounded by regions of localized ones, and the eigenvalues are all confined to an annular region. Furthermore, the distinct band structures of the SSH chain and ladder model lead to different delocalization phenomena. Card 02 摘要

摘要

  • 构造two-band non-Hermitian SSH chain与ladder，随机column signs满足简化Dale's law，directional bias g造成左右非互易 hopping。
  • \(g=0\) random signs使states localized；增大g按Hatano–Nelson机制在complex spectrum上生成extended-state loops。
  • SSH pure band在g_c处有order-2 exceptional point，disorder后零本征值仍只有一个eigenvector；ladder对应的是有两个正交eigenvectors的diabolic point。
  • SSH extended loops最终合并并打开中心hole；ladder先产生outer loops，再在g>g_c出现inner loop，中间仍夹localized states。
  • transfer-matrix Lyapunov exponents给extended contours \(g=\gamma_{i}\)，与large-matrix diagonalization/IPR着色吻合。
Card 03 背景

背景

  • 神经连接矩阵天然非对称，Dale's law要求同一presynaptic neuron的outgoing signs一致，ring attractors与distance-decaying connectivity又给band sparsity。
  • linearized rate network为τdv/dt=−v+\(h\)+Mv；本文只分析M的谱与eigenvector localization，未处理activation saturation。
  • non-Hermitian directional bias可抵消disorder localization；exceptional point是eigenvalues与eigenvectors同时合并，diabolic point仅eigenvalues简并。
  • 两band substructure让单band Hatano–Nelson图景出现多阶段delocalization和不同零点简并。
Card 04 模型与方法

模型与方法

  • SSH每unit cell有A/B，intracell/intercell \(t\)±=\(t\pm \deltat\)，forward/backward乘e^{±\(g\)}；每site独立σ_{j,A/B}=±1并乘在相应outgoing column。
  • ladder有两条相同biased chains、leg hopping t与rung coupling \(u\)，再施加相同column-sign disorder；两模型主要用periodic boundaries。
  • 因M=HΣ且Σ是diagonal unitary sign matrix，disordered M与clean H有相同singular values，故所有eigenvalue modulus落在s_min≤|\(\lambda\)|≤\(s_{\mathrm{max}}\) annulus。
  • non-Hermitian IPR用left/right product：\(\Sigma_{i}\)|\(\psi_{L}\)^*(i)\(\psi_{R}\)(i)|²/[\(\Sigma_{i}\)|\(\psi_{L}\)^*(i)\(\psi_{R}\)(i)|]²，避免单边gauge dependence。
  • SSH用Riccati recursion取单一Lyapunov exponent；ladder用4×4 random transfer products的singular growth取γ1>γ2。
  • direct diagonalization主图常用N=5000 unit cells；Lyapunov contours用N=10000或5000 transfer steps并丢初始10%。
Card 05 核心结果与证据

核心结果与证据

  • clean SSH point gap在g_c=\(atanh(\deltat/t)=0\).5log(\(t\)+/\(t\)−)闭合，\(k=\pi\) block变Jordan form；even N的random-sign SSH仍保留twofold \(\lambda\)=0但单eigenvector。
  • SSH singular bounds解析给s_min²=2t+\(t\)−[cosh(2|\(g\)−\(g_{c}\)|)−1]；\(g\)=g_c时inner radius为0。
  • Figures 4–5中t=1、\(\deltat=0.25\)、\(N=5000\)：四个extended loops从各band内部出现，在g_c≈0.255于origin合并，随后所有states逐步delocalize。
  • SSH transfer calculation给g=0 localization landscape；有限g extended eigenvalues精确落在γ(\(\lambda\))=\(g\) contour，且γ(0)=\(g_{c}\)−\(g\)。
  • clean ladder E±(\(k\))=2t[cosh \(g\) cosk+i sinh \(g\) sink]±\(u\)，在g_c=arcosh(\(u\)/2t)于k=0、π产生两个独立zero modes，故为diabolic。
  • random ladder singular bounds为s_min=|\(u\)−2t cosh \(g\)|、\(s_{\mathrm{max}}=u\)+2t cosh \(g\)；even N的two-dimensional zero eigenspace survives sign disorder。
  • Figures 9–11中t=1、\(u=2.5\)：\(g\)\(g_{c}\approx 0\).693后γ1=\(g\) inner contours也出现，形成两loops夹localized band。
  • open boundaries可用diagonal gauge消去g的eigenvalue effect，right/left eigenvectors分别pile at opposite edges，产生skin effect；SSH在t+<\(t\)−还保留near-zero edge modes。
Card 06 有效性与局限

有效性与局限

  • Dale's law只实现column sign一致且E/I各半，coupling magnitudes仍由理想SSH/ladder决定；真实network的cell types、degree、self-coupling和balance未建模。
  • 网络是one-dimensional periodic sparse topology与two-site unit cell，远简于connectome；neural interpretation主要是motivation而非empirical validation。
  • 只分析linear connectivity spectrum，network stability还受−I shift、gain F'、inputs与nonlinear saturation影响；delocalized eigenmode不等于neural computation或activity pattern。
  • 随机sign没有magnitude disorder；局域化与degeneracy对continuous weights、unequal E/I fractions和longer-range edges的稳健性未系统测试。
  • IPR与spectra展示有限N single/few disorder realizations；需给ensemble distributions和finite-size scaling才可精确定位transition。
  • Lyapunov method对任意complex λ都输出γ，即使那里没有eigenvalue；必须与spectral support联合解释。
  • periodic-boundary delocalization与open-boundary skin effect截然不同；不能把PBC extended loops直接外推到finite biological ring。
  • zero degeneracy survives需要even N和严格结构；edge modes也依t+<\(t\)−与boundary termination。
  • left/right eigenvector product IPR适合biorthogonal localization，但near exceptional point normalization和nonorthogonality可病态。
Card 07 复现与资源

复现与资源

  • 生成M时逐column验证所有nonzero outgoing entries share \(\sigma_{j}\)，并检查M=HΣ、\(\Sigma\)†\(\Sigma\)=I及clean/disordered singular values逐点一致。
  • 对SSH扫描g跨g_c，计算eigenvalue、algebraic/geometric multiplicity、Jordan rank与biorthogonal IPR，区分exceptional和普通zero crossing。
  • 对ladder同样验证two-dimensional zero eigenspace，并以condition number/Schur decomposition避免近简并数值误判。
  • transfer iteration用periodic QR stabilization和多random seeds，报告γ_i mean/error；把γ_i=\(g\) contours与N-scaling IPR交叉验证。
  • 做N=500到20000 finite-size scaling，测extended-loop thickness、IPR、zero-mode splitting与localized fraction。
  • 分别增加magnitude disorder、E/I imbalance、longer-range hopping和dense intra-cell blocks，检验two-stage ladder mechanism的结构稳健性。
  • open boundaries下验证eigenvalues对g invariant、right/left center-of-mass向相反edges移动，并区分topological edge mode与skin accumulation。

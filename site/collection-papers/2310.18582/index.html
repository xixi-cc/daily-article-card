TITLE · ABSTRACT

Data-driven learning of the generalized Langevin equation with state-dependent memory

具有状态依赖记忆的广义Langevin方程数据驱动学习

We present a data-driven method to learn stochastic reduced models of complex systems that retain a state-dependent memory beyond the standard generalized Langevin equation with a homogeneous kernel. The constructed model naturally encodes heterogeneous energy dissipation by jointly learning a set of state features and the non-Markovian coupling among the features. Numerical results demonstrate the limitation of the standard GLE and the essential role of state dependency in predicting molecule kinetics related to conformation relaxation and transition.

Card 02 摘要

摘要

  • Mori–Zwanzig投影的一般memory \(K\)(\(q\),\(t\))可依赖resolved state；standard GLE把它简化成仅依赖lag的homogeneous kernel，可能抹去构象附近不同耗散。
  • 作者将memory写成φ(\(q_{t}\))^TΘ(\(t\)−\(\tau\))\(\phi\)(q_τ)，用多个state features编码空间异质性、matrix kernel编码feature间延迟耦合。
  • 用按初始q*条件化的三点correlations训练φ和Θ，因为ordinary two-point autocorrelation只看到全局平均memory。
  • 按second fluctuation–dissipation relation构造coherent Gaussian noise，保持canonical invariant density；FFT与fast convolution使长轨迹模拟O(NlogN)。
  • benzyl-bromide例中四feature full-kernel模型恢复conditional correlations、relaxation和transition-time distribution，而homogeneous GLE显著预测过快动力学。
Card 03 背景

背景

  • 投影full Hamiltonian dynamics到少数coarse variables后，unresolved degrees产生free-energy force、history-dependent friction与colored noise。
  • 标准GLE常由velocity autocorrelation反演单一K(\(t\))，能匹配被选二点统计却不保证不同metastable basins与saddle附近的局域动力学。
  • 构象转变对barrier region friction极敏感；同一global average kernel可低估saddle耗散，从而错误提高short transition probability。
  • 本文不直接求昂贵orthogonal dynamics，而问什么可学习model class能保留state-dependent non-Markovian signatures。

模型与方法

  • resolved \(q\),p满足q̇=\(M\)^−1p，\(p\)̇=−\(\nabla U(q)\)−∫\(\phi_{t}\)^TΘ(\(t\)−\(\tau\))φ_τv_τdτ+\(R_{t}\)；本文选constant M并假定U已知。
  • features \(\phi_{i}(q)=H_{i}\)^Tψ(\(q\))由piecewise-linear sparse bases组成；kernel \(\Theta(t)=e\)^−αtΣ_kΘ̂_kcosω_kt，\(\Theta\)̂_k=Γ_kΓ_k^T保证positive semidefinite。
  • 训练metric来自g(\(t\);\(q\)*)=E{[\(p\)̇_t+\(\nabla U(q_{t})\)]\(v_{0}^{T}\)|\(q_{0}=q\)*}与model convolution；所需C_{\(\psi\),\(\psi\)}(\(t\),\(\tau\);\(q\)*)是conditional three-point correlation。
  • 稀疏ψ让basis products O(1)评估并预计算correlations；优化H、lower-triangular Γ_k与α，另加overall two-point loss作两阶段训练。
  • noise写R_t=\(\phi_{t}^{T}\) R̃(\(t\))，协方差k_BT e^−\(\alpha\)|\(t\)−\(\tau\)|\(\Theta\)(|\(t\)−\(\tau\)|)，从而与memory匹配并保持ρ_eq∝exp[−\(\beta\)(U+\(p^{\mathrm{TM}}\)^−1p/2)]。
  • 测试系统含一benzyl bromide加2400 waters，AMBER/RESP、TIP3P、PME与SHAKE；298 \(K\)、1 bar平衡16 ns后NVT production 2.5 μs。
  • q是Br到ipso-carbon距离，U(\(q\))=−k_BTlnρ(\(q\))由MD kernel density estimate得到；〈\(q\)∇U〉=0.996k_BT作内部检查。

Card 06 有效性与局限

有效性与局限

  • Eq. (3)被作者明确称为保留state-dependent memory的model，而非Zwanzig exact kernel的直接近似；learned features未必有唯一microscopic interpretation。
  • U(\(q\))从同一2.5 μs MD估计且先验已知；free-energy uncertainty与memory-learning误差未联合传播。
  • 单一distance q可能遗漏其他slow coordinates，SD-GLE通过memory补偿但不能证明chosen coordinate充分。
  • conditional correlations与transition statistics似乎来自同一MD production pool；未清楚展示严格train/test trajectory split或跨force-field transfer。
  • 只研究一个小分子、一个温度与一条reaction coordinate，四features的普适性和sample complexity未知。
  • transition events可能稀少且彼此相关；正文未给distribution confidence intervals、event count或rare-tail calibration。
  • positive-semidefinite cosine kernel和symmetric assumption是model restriction；α接近零主要作regularizer，有限cutoff会影响long memory。
  • canonical invariant density由matched noise构造保证，但numerical discretization、finite FFT period与estimated kernel可能引入bias。
  • standard GLE baseline通过global correlations建立；其它state-dependent friction或nonlinear GLE baselines有限，不能把提升唯一归因于该parameterization。
  • 本站未运行MD、训练或transition resampling。
Card 07 复现与资源

复现与资源

  • 复现先冻结MD force field、thermostat、box、sampling stride与q定义；按independent time blocks划train/validation/test，防止long-memory leakage。
  • 报告每个q* bin的occupancy、effective samples和conditional-correlation uncertainty；saddle附近必须有足够coverage。
  • 从standard GLE、Markov \(\gamma\)(\(q\))、one-feature、multi-feature diagonal Θ到full Θ逐级ablation，并用held-out correlations与transition events共同选模。
  • U(\(q\))用block bootstrap重新估计，将free-energy曲线误差传到relaxation/first-passage distribution；不要把所有偏差归memory。
  • 验证FDT与numerics：测stationary \(q\),\(p\) density、equipartition、noise covariance、energy dissipation和time-step/FFT-window convergence。
  • transition统计公开event definition、recrossing处理、censoring、number of transitions及survival curve confidence bands。
  • 扫描feature count、basis resolution、kernel modes与cutoff，报告validation plateau而非只展示n=4。
  • 跨温度或不同solvent做held-out transfer可检验features是否编码真实heterogeneous coupling还是单dataset插值。

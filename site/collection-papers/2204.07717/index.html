Emergence of Self-dual Patterns in Active Colloids with Periodical Feedback to Local Density

局域密度周期反馈下主动胶体自对偶图样的涌现

The microscopic dynamics of self-organization for patterns is investigated and quantified in a periodically propelled Quincke system. The periodical coupling between propulsion and repulsion at the particle level leads to local directed oscillating particle flows and promises a loop of positive feedback to density fluctuations. Nevertheless, the global evolution of the resulting cluster phase is dominated by a global dual transformation. As stable attractors of the dual transformation, self-dual patterns including stripe patterns and square lattices can be achieved by tuning the strength and the frequency of propelling. However, stripes are possible only at strong propelling where boundary particle flows can form. Card 02 摘要

摘要

  • 方波电场在每周期前半开启Quincke propulsion与dipolar repulsion、后半关闭；初始密度涨落被停机冻结，再在下一开启段从dense domains定向输运到voids。
  • 局域normal flows放大density contrast并形成disordered clusters；多个周期后cluster centers趋向上一周期Voronoi diagram的vertices，形成作者称为dual transformation的更新。
  • 中等频率得到两套相互交换的square lattices，低频且E_p>1.9E_c时boundary tangential flows拉长clusters并连接成stripes。
  • 更高频path length太短而呈homogeneous gas，更低频on-time太长让stopped particles reactivation并转为flocking。
  • 方格/条纹的density和velocity autocorrelations在驱动频率一半处有峰，即响应周期2T_f；论文将其描述为classical time-crystal approach。
Card 03 背景

背景

  • 不同反应扩散、流体与颗粒模型可生成相似图样，仅靠拟合宏观pattern难以识别真实微观机制；可逐粒跟踪的active colloids提供直接证据。
  • Quincke rotation超过threshold E_c后给近constant rolling speed；square-wave同时门控propulsion和field-induced repulsion，天然实现activity–density的周期反馈。
  • 先前periodically propelled Quincke实验已发现dynamic square lattice，但条纹为何缺失、无序cluster如何选择规则排列尚未解释。
  • 本文把local instability、global center relocation和boundary-flow shape instability分为不同尺度的机制，而非用单一linear instability概括全部演化。
Card 04 模型与方法

模型与方法

  • 直径4.90 μm polystyrene spheres分散于0.15 \(M\) AOT/hexadecane，两ITO玻璃间施加50% duty-cycle square wave：E在E_p>E_c与0之间切换。
  • 显微成像500或1000 fps，tracking精度0.1 pixel；速度v约随√[(\(E_{p}\)/\(E_{c}\))²−1]变化，每个on-half-cycle path length \(S=vT_{f}\)/\(2=v\)/(2f)。
  • cluster particles定义为local area fraction高于global mean \(\phi_{0}\)，cluster center取其坐标平均；当前centers做Voronoi tessellation，vertices与下一周期centers比较。
  • density field按cluster radius尺度网格化；计算local density autocorrelation C_φ(\(\tau\))与unit-velocity autocorrelation \(C_{v}\)(\(\tau\))，并取Fourier spectra。
  • 局域flow用n=−\(\nabla \phi\)/|\(\nabla \phi\)|分成normal \(v_{n}=v\)·n和tangential \(v_{t}=v\)−\(v_{\mathrm{nn}}\)；同时跟踪active-particle fraction ϕ以分辨clustering、boundary flow和reactivation。
  • square-lattice cluster radius R_c及nearest/next-nearest center peaks给lattice constant L与√2L；随T_f追踪几何scale。
  • \(E_{p}\)–\(f\) phase diagram以final state分类homogeneous gas、square lattice、stripe和flocking，并用多组movies展示transients。
Card 05 核心结果与证据

核心结果与证据

  • Figure 1显示E_p=1.8E_c、30 Hz时均匀态先成无序clusters，数百周期后成为交替square lattices；\(E_{p}=2\).0E_c、5 Hz则先clusters elongation后成为交替stripes。
  • Figure 2中square lattice覆盖所探索E_p的一段中频区；stripe只在E_p>1.9E_c且较低频出现，高频为homogeneous、极低频为flocking。
  • S小于mean neighbor spacing d时motion localized、density fluctuations受抑；很长on-time则允许velocity alignment与continuous collective motion，给两端状态物理尺度。
  • Figure 3a–d中disordered阶段下一周期centers只近似落在Voronoi vertices，steady square阶段overlap更精确；self-dual是这一center→Voronoi-vertex map下的operational fixed-pattern property。
  • square lattice中clusters与voids逐周期交换；C_φ与C_v spectra在f/2有响应，且density radial peaks给L、√2L，支持square symmetry与period doubling。
  • R_c和L在square regime随T_f近线性增大，进入stripe区后线性关系中断，说明path length控制scale但不足以决定morphology。
  • Figure 4在16 Hz square case显示clustering时|\(v_{n}\)|>|\(v_{t}\)|且active fraction持续下降；在5 Hz stripe case，ϕ出现plateau且cluster boundary上|\(v_{t}\)|占优。
  • 非均匀boundary flows沿cluster edge输运粒子，使cluster在dominant direction延长并连接；steady stripes仍保留BFs，支持其既促成又维持stripe。
  • \(E_{p}=2\).1E_c时boundary-flow redirecting time τ_f约10 ms；作者以τ_c<τ_f给square、\(\tau_{f}\)<\(\tau_{c}\)<τ_a给stripe、\(\tau_{c}\)>τ_a给flocking的timescale ordering组织phase selection。
Card 06 有效性与局限

有效性与局限

  • Voronoi vertices落入next-cycle clusters是几何相关证据；没有建立一个由microscopic equations严格推出、可独立预测transition probabilities的dual-map动力学。
  • ‘stable attractor’主要由最终图样长期视觉稳定和overlap支持，未给basin volume、perturbation recovery或Lyapunov spectrum。
  • square/stripe self-duality保持的是symmetry与structure constants，不是每粒子、每cluster的一一映射；finite defects和misassigned centers会影响Voronoi audit。
  • half-frequency peak证明period doubling，但强time-crystal主张通常还需frequency rigidity、long-lived subharmonic response、对noise/perturbation稳健及size scaling；本文未完整给出。
  • \(\tau_{c}\)、\(\tau_{f}\)、τ_a是影像中提取的operational times，受E_p、cluster size、density和threshold影响；phase inequalities是机制框架而非闭式相界。
  • stripe threshold \(E_{p}\approx 1\).9E_c只在φ_0=0.30及特定溶剂/particle setup测得，不具普适性。
  • normal/tangential decomposition在|\(\nabla \phi\)|小处不稳定，局部smoothing与binning细节可能影响v_t dominance。
  • field同时改变speed、dipolar interaction和electrohydrodynamic flows，单独归因propulsion strength需要额外独立控制。
  • 实验主要提供selected state points、images与movies，phase diagram的replicate counts、uncertainty和hysteresis未完整报告。
  • 本站未检查原始movies或重算Voronoi center matching。
Card 07 复现与资源

复现与资源

  • 复现实验逐cycle保存field trigger、particle tracks、density grid、cluster labels和centers；所有times与T_f同步，防止camera/field phase drift伪造subharmonic。
  • Voronoi test对每周期计算vertices到next centers的nearest-distance distribution，并与随机平移、same-cycle及density-max baselines比较，而不只展示overlaid snapshots。
  • time-crystal审计需在多个f、\(E_{p}\)、system size与noise下测subharmonic linewidth、lifetime、phase slips、detuning locking range和perturbation recovery。
  • flow mechanism逐cluster保存v_n、\(v_{t}\)、ϕ、perimeter和elongation tensor；用event-level analysis检验BF onset是否先于shape elongation。
  • \(\tau_{c}\)、\(\tau_{f}\)、τ_a用预注册阈值提取并bootstrap across clusters/runs；直接测试三种time ordering对held-out state label的预测准确率。
  • 独立改变duty cycle可在固定peak E_p下分离on-time/path length和off-time freezing；改变φ_0检验1.9E_c threshold与Voronoi selection。
  • 对density smoothing、cluster cutoff和Voronoi boundary treatment做敏感性分析，并保留homogeneous/flocking transitions的失败pattern runs。

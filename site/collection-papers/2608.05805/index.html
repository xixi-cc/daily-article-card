Large Spin-Wave Fluctuations Suppress Activity in Malthusian Flocks

大自旋波涨落抑制马尔萨斯鸟群中的活性

Novel phases, beyond long-range order in two dimensions, have continued to be discovered within flocking models, establishing flocking as one of the pivotal paradigms in active matter. However, much of the discussion around ``Malthusian'' (constant density) flocks, an analytically more tractable alternative to the Vicsek model, has centred around the scaling exponents governing the intermediate regime prior to the proliferation of asters, leaving open the question of what other phases the model might display. Here, we study the two-dimensional dynamics of Malthusian flocks and identify a previously unnoticed phase, where the dynamics is that of the equilibrium XY Model. By identifying the symmetries of the model, we derive the effective equations of motion for the Goldstone modes and analyse the spin-wave fluctuations. Card 02 摘要

摘要

  • Malthusian flock通过birth/death把density变成fast variable，只保留velocity/order-parameter field，兼具简化解析性与active space–spin coupling。
  • 在ordered phase以compact angle θ表示Goldstone mode，由restricted \(\mathrm{O}(2)\) symmetry枚举二阶gradient Langevin equation。
  • 二维Gaussian spin waves给trigonometric activity vertex额外anomalous dimension，使naive relevant的advection λ在高noise下变得irrelevant。
  • effective theory约化为θdot=Dλ(cosθ∂\(x\)+sinθ∂\(y\))\(\theta\)+\(D\)∇²θ+√(2Γ)\(\xi\)。
  • 在δ=\(\Gamma\)/(4πD)−1与λ附近的leading RG flow类似BKT：一侧流向λ=0的vortex-free equilibrium XY line，另一侧流向强耦合Malthusian phase。
Card 03 背景

背景

  • 二维Vicsek flock可突破平衡Mermin–Wagner限制，但完整density–velocity hydrodynamics及scaling exponents长期有争议。
  • Malthusian flock假设birth/death快速松弛density，留下单一vector field；数值此前显示asters最终破坏潜在long-range order，只在中间scale观察universal scaling。
  • vision-cone nonreciprocal XY models据称与Malthusian continuum属同一universality class，因活动把order-parameter rotation与physical-space rotation绑定。
  • 本文不再只问active fixed-point exponents，而问noise是否能把activity operator屏蔽掉，从而出现有效平衡相。
Card 04 模型与方法

模型与方法

  • order parameter ϕ=(ϕ0+\(\sigma\))(cosθ,sinθ)，σ是heavy amplitude、θ是compact Goldstone；只研究d=2 symmetry-broken sector。
  • restricted O(2)要求field和space同时旋转，另有parity \(\theta\)→−\(\theta\)、\(y\)→−\(y\)；由parallel/perpendicular derivatives构造允许项。
  • 完整二阶方程含D、\(D\)-tilde、\(g\)-tilde和sin2θ/cos2θ vertices；Gaussian spin-wave scaling使anisotropic \(D\)-tilde与g-tilde更irrelevant，留下单一active \(\lambda\)。
  • free theoryequal-time correlator给〈\(e^{in\)[\(\theta\)(\(x\))−\(\theta\)(0)]}〉∝|\(x\)|^{−n²Γ/(2πD)}，所以cosθ带dimension \(\Gamma\)/(4πD)。
  • λ的effective length dimension为L^{−1+\(\Gamma\)/(4πD)}；\(\Gamma\)>4πD时线性relevance反转，Gaussian critical point在λ=0、\(\delta=0\)。
  • 以MSRJD action做perturbative renormalization；由于sinusoidal vertex产生无穷多项，展开按λ、δ而非普通loop order组织。
Card 05 核心结果与证据

核心结果与证据

  • leading flow为μ \(d\lambda_{R}\)/dμ=δ_Rλ_R、\(\mu\) \(d\delta_{R}\)/dμ=(\(\alpha\)/4)λ_R²，\(\alpha=0.62433\)…；μ下降代表流向IR。
  • \(\delta_{R}\)>0且|\(\lambda_{R}\)|≤2δ_R/√α时，flow进入λ_R=0 fixed line；大spin-wave noise令activity asymptotically irrelevant，dynamics落在vortex-free XY class。
  • \(\delta_{R}\)<0或δ_R>0但|\(\lambda_{R}\)|超过separatrix时，λ_R最终增长，定义active Malthusian sector；其fixed point不在perturbative控制内。
  • 两相由half-stable Gaussian critical point分隔，只能沿δ_R=\(\sqrt \alpha\)|\(\lambda_{R}\)|/2、\(\delta_{R}\)>0 separatrix到达。
  • flow topology经λ'_R=\(\sqrt \alpha\) \(\lambda_{R}\)/2后与BKT方程同形，但物理不同：这里是spin waves和activity竞争，平衡BKT则由vortex unbinding。
  • separatrix上δ_R≈−1/lnℓ，spin correlation预测〈cos[\(\theta\)(\(x\),\(t\))−\(\theta\)(0,0)]〉=|\(x\)|^{-2}\(ln^{-2}\)|\(x\)|乘带log-corrected time argument的scaling function。
  • 结论中的‘XY phase’是λ流向0的large-scale dynamics，不是说microscopic detailed balance从未破缺；activity operator在IR不可见。
  • 作者的Note added指出同期Grosvenor–Patil工作得出不同阈值Γ<2πD并称D无divergent correction，说明关键RG结论尚有公开分歧。
Card 06 有效性与局限

有效性与局限

  • 分析主动排除vortices并假设θ处处differentiable，只覆盖spin-wave sector；compact field真实会有topological defects，完整phase diagram还需vortex fugacity轴。
  • Malthusian strong-coupling phase的fixed point不可由本文perturbative RG访问，故没有给其exponents、aster physics或最终稳态。
  • RG只保留λ、\(\delta\) leading order；sinusoidal interaction在任意λ order可含arbitrarily many loops，renormalisability是critical-point附近的假设。
  • 从symmetry直接写Goldstone EoM避免integrating out amplitude遗漏terms，但couplings与具体microscopic Malthusian model没有定量匹配。
  • \(D\)-tilde与g-tilde被判irrelevant基于Gaussian scaling；靠近strong coupling或vortices时可能需要重新审计。
  • 没有numerical integration、Monte Carlo、particle simulation或data collapse验证phase boundary和log corrections；Figure 1只是RG方程流线。
  • 同期开源理论给不同activity threshold与D renormalization，论文自身明确记录冲突；需独立diagrammatic或functional RG核对。
  • 高noise下λ irrelevant同时可能促进vortices并毁掉quasi-long-range order；称equilibrium XY phase必须限定为vortex-free slice。
  • density确实fast/constant是Malthusian假设；不能直接外推到Vicsek model的coupled slow density。
  • 作者推测vortices会更强抑制activity，但这不是本文显式计算结果。

Nonequilbrium physics of generative diffusion models

生成扩散模型的非平衡物理：熵产生、涨落定理与相变

Generative diffusion models apply the concept of Langevin dynamics in physics to machine leaning, attracting a lot of interests from engineering, statistics and physics, but a complete picture about inherent mechanisms is still lacking. In this paper, we provide a transparent physics analysis of diffusion models, formulating the fluctuation theorem, entropy production, equilibrium measure, and Franz-Parisi potential to understand the dynamic process and intrinsic phase transitions. Our analysis is rooted in a path integral representation of both forward and backward dynamics, and in treating the reverse diffusion generative process as a statistical inference, where the time-dependent state variables serve as quenched disorder akin to that in spin glass theory.   • 推导含义：路径概率比给出总熵产生；积分涨落定理进一步推出平均熵产生非负。
  • 结论边界：训练/score 误差改变反向路径测度，因此可被解释为额外不可逆性。

  • 论证节点“反向扩散也是带 quenched disorder 的统计推断”。
  • 给定当前 noisy state，反向过程推断其可能的数据源。
  • 时间依赖状态在分析中类似自旋玻璃的 quenched disorder。
  • Franz–Parisi potential 描述候选状态与参考样本 overlap 的自由能景观。
  • 单井、对称破缺与多稳态结构对应不同推断阶段与可恢复性。
  • score dynamics 与能量景观提供了同一生成过程的局部和全局描述。
  • 论文将 reverse diffusion 重写为统计推断并引入 Franz–Parisi potential。 （\(p\). 10; source/tex/npdm.tex:370-600）

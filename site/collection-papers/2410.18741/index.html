TITLE · ABSTRACT

Giant density fluctuations in locally hyperuniform states

局域超均匀态中的巨密度涨落

Systems driven far from equilibrium may exhibit anomalous density fluctuations: active matter with orientational order display giant density fluctuations at large scale, while systems of interacting particles close to an absorbing phase transition may exhibit hyperuniformity, suppressing large-scale density fluctuations. We show that these seemingly incompatible phenomena can coexist in nematically ordered active systems, provided activity is conditioned to particle contacts. We characterize this unusual state and unravel mechanisms simultaneously leading to spatially enhanced fluctuations on large length scales and suppressed fluctuations on intermediate length scales. Our work highlights rich phenomenology in active matter systems in which particles' activity is triggered by their local environment.

Card 02 摘要

摘要

  • Nematic Random Organization Model（NROM）让所有particle directors按nematic Vicsek rule更新，但只有与另一粒子overlap者沿自身director随机正/负步进。
  • 模型同时保留ROM-like absorbing transition和active-nematic orientational order；所选参数在临界点上方steady state中S>0.9。
  • number variance在intermediate boxes呈α_hu≈0.77<1，在larger boxes呈α_gnf≈1.65>1；同一configuration随coarse-graining scale显得先更均匀、后更粗糙。
  • crossover length ξ随Δφ=\(\phi\)−φ_c趋零而发散，数值collapse给μ≈0.625；angular-averaged structure factor给相同q*=\(\xi\)^−1 crossover。
  • continuum theory把它解释为activity-field noise在intermediate q抑制density，而nematic curvature noise在small q产生GNF。
Card 03 背景

背景

  • Hyperuniformity要求q→0时S(\(q\))→0或大窗口number variance低于体积标度；absorbing-state random organization常在临界点附近产生这种抑制。
  • 有orientational order的active matter因density与Goldstone/director fluctuations耦合，通常在small q出现S(\(q\))发散和giant number fluctuations，无需fine tuning。
  • 二者表面矛盾，是因为标准定义都指largest scales；本文的标题用‘locally hyperuniform’强调抑制只发生在有限的intermediate scale window。
  • neighbor-triggered motility可见于cyclically sheared rods、subcritical Quincke rollers、stress-responsive cells和sensing microrobots，因此组合机制具有实验动机。
Card 04 模型与方法

模型与方法

  • 二维periodic box中直径D粒子携带axial angle \(\theta\)∈[−\(\pi\)/2,\(\pi\)/2]；每步对range R_al邻居平均exp(i2θ)，加uniform noise \(\psi\)∈[−σπ/2,σπ/2]。
  • 仅center distance
  • 主参数σ=0.1、\(\delta_{0}=0.3\)、\(R_{\mathrm{al}}=1\)、\(D=0.46\)，L通常516或靠近临界用774；随机positions/angles初始化并多次实现平均。
  • activity A为overlapping fraction，nematic order S由traceless tensor Q计算；activity decay/plateau拟合φ_c=\(0.30984\pm 0\).00006及β≈0.63。
  • number variance对box sizeℓ测〈Δn²〉_ℓ与〈\(n\)〉_ℓ；structure factor先计算S(\(q\))再angular average \(S_{\mathrm{iso}}\)(\(q\))，同时保留nematic anisotropy caveat。
  • 三场理论用conserved densityρ、nonconserved overlap density A和nematic tensor \(Q\)；同一particle current含D_ρ∇A及active-nematic curvature terms。
  • 围绕A_0∝Δφ、\(Q_{0}\ne 0\) homogeneous state线性化，解析S(\(q\),\(\theta\))并识别activity与nematic两类effective noises。
Card 05 核心结果与证据

核心结果与证据

  • Figure 1c–e中φ>φ_c的A趋正plateau、\(\phi\)<φ_c有限时归零；activity exponent与2D conserved directed percolation接近，但作者未完成universality-class证明。
  • 在所有分析的active-side states，nematic S>0.9且其relaxation time不在φ_c发散，说明吸收临界性发生在已建立orientation order的背景。
  • Figure 2a给α_hu=\(0.77\pm 0\).005与α_gnf=\(1.65\pm 0\).05两个number-variance slopes；两值分别接近ROM和2D nematic Vicsek既有结果。
  • number variance collapse使用μ=\(0.625\pm 0.02\)、\(\zeta=0.75\pm 0.05\)；\(\xi\sim \Delta\phi\)^−μ意味着趋近φ_c时hyperuniform-like window延伸到更大尺度。
  • Figure 2c–d中small q的S_iso发散、intermediate q的S_iso随q上升；collapse给μ=\(0.625\pm 0.05\)、\(\zeta\)′=\(0.55\pm 0.05\)，与ζ′=2μ−ζ近似一致。
  • Figure 3同一NROM configuration在s=3比Poisson reference更平滑，在s=30却有更大、更长程的density patches，直观看到尺度反转。
  • linear theory Eq. (7)预言q̃=qξ下S/\(\Delta\phi^{1}\)/2含q̃^−2 nematic项与q̃² activity项，给theory \(\mu=3\)/4、\(\zeta\)′=1/2；前者与数值0.625有可见偏差。
  • 在q≫\(q\)*，activity noise给S∼q²/\(A_{0}\)；\(q\)≪\(q\)*时nematic tensor noise给S∼(1−cos4θ)A_0²/q²，说明large-scale GNF还具有fourfold angular dependence。
  • absorbing side因nematic order来不及形成，supplement显示q→0转为normal fluctuations并只残留intermediate-\(q\) hyperuniformity，没有GNF。
Card 06 有效性与局限

有效性与局限

  • 任何有限Δφ>0下q→0由GNF主导，因此该steady state不满足严格global hyperuniform定义；‘local’指有限/intermediate length scales。
  • number-variance exponents来自有限scaling windows，crossover和system size会偏置slopes；最大L仍限制最小q。
  • φ_c与β取决于σ、\(\delta_{0}\)、\(D\)/\(R_{\mathrm{al}}\)；β接近CDP不是包含nematic field后universality不变的充分证明。
  • continuum theory假定linearly stable homogeneous nematic state并线性化；nonlinearities、defects、bands和critical renormalization被忽略。
  • theory \(\mu=0\).75与simulation \(\mu\approx 0\).625不相等，作者称qualitative/reasonably close；不能报成定量预测成功。
  • \(S_{\mathrm{iso}}\) angular average隐藏Eq. (7)预言的1−cos4θ anisotropy，正文未做完整direction-resolved exponent test。
  • 模型中overlap与motility触发是离散硬阈值、步长固定且沿director正负等概率；具体实验会有continuous response和hydrodynamics。
  • absorbing simulations在activity归零时停止，nematic order的潜在长时间角度演化与frozen positions需另行定义。
  • very-low-density或附加single-particle motility会消除strict absorbing state并截断critical crossover。
  • 本站未运行数百万步模拟或独立复核fit windows。
Card 07 复现与资源

复现与资源

  • 复现先逐L定位φ_c：保存A(\(t\))、survival probability、absorption time与S(\(t\))，并以多seed/finite-size scaling区分active plateau和long transient。
  • 在φ>\(\phi_{c}\) steady state用log-spacedℓ测number variance，报告window placement、boundary wrapping、overlap correlations和slope local derivative，不只做global two-line fit。
  • S(\(q\))同时输出S(\(q_{x}\),\(q_{y}\))、parallel/perpendicular/angular average；检验fourfold factor和q*是否在不同direction一致。
  • collapse拟合联合估计μ、ζ及不确定度，改变fit window和排除最小q点；把theory \(\mu=0\).75作为falsifiable comparator。
  • 用同一configuration做多个Gaussian coarse-grain s并与Poisson、ordinary ROM、always-active nematic三种matched controls比较。
  • 机制消融分别关闭Q curvature coupling或取消contact gate，确认前者移除small-\(q\) GNF、后者移除intermediate-\(q\) suppression。
  • 扫描σ、\(\delta_{0}\)、\(D\)/R_al与额外spontaneous motility，测crossover是否被cut off并建立可实验的robustness map。

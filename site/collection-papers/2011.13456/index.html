TITLE · ABSTRACT

Score-Based Generative Modeling through Stochastic Differential Equations

基于随机微分方程的分数生成建模

Creating noise from data is easy; creating data from noise is generative modeling. We present a stochastic differential equation that smoothly transforms a complex data distribution to a known prior distribution by slowly injecting noise, and a corresponding reverse-time SDE that transforms the prior distribution back into the data distribution by slowly removing the noise. Crucially, the reverse-time SDE depends only on the time-dependent gradient field, or score, of the perturbed data distribution.

Card 02 摘要

摘要

  • forward Itô SDE \(dx=f(x,t)dt\)+\(g\)(\(t\))dw把data p0连续扰动到tractable prior pT；reverse-time drift多出−g²∇\(x\) log pt(\(x\))，因此只要估计各时刻score即可从噪声生成。
  • continuous denoising score matching以已知Gaussian perturbation kernel训练sθ(\(x\),\(t\))；VE对应SMLD极限，VP对应DDPM极限，sub-VP为本文为likelihood提出的变体。
  • predictor-corrector把reverse-SDE数值步与Langevin等MCMC校正交替；probability-flow ODE与SDE共享每个时刻的marginal densities，并允许density evaluation与deterministic encoding。
Card 03 背景

背景

  • SMLD在多个noise scales上学score并用annealed Langevin采样；DDPM的noise-prediction/variational objective也可重写为加权denoising score objectives。
  • 连续SDE框架把noise schedule、training objective和sampling discretization解耦，使通用SDE solvers、ancestral/reverse-diffusion、PC与ODE sampling可在同一score model上组合。
  • 关键对象是marginal score \(\nabla log\) pt，而不是单样本forward conditional score本身；denoising identity保证对conditional target做回归的population optimum等于marginal score。
Card 04 模型与方法

模型与方法

  • reverse SDE为dx=[\(f\)−g²∇log pt]dt+\(g\) \(d\) w-bar，时间从T到0；公式成立依赖适当regularity与真实marginal score。实际用sθ替代后同时有estimation和discretization error。
  • 训练Eq. (7)均匀采t，\(x_{0}\)~pdata、xt~p0t(.|\(x_{0}\))，最小化λ(\(t\))||sθ(xt,\(t\))−\(\nabla xt\) log p0t(xt|\(x_{0}\))||²；充分数据/容量/优化下才恢复score。
  • VE SDE无drift且variance随σ²(\(t\))增长；VP为Ornstein–Uhlenbeck型drift −\(\beta\)(\(t\))\(x\)/2、diffusion \(\sqrt \beta\)；sub-VP降低intermediate diffusion variance。三者affine drift使transition Gaussian闭式可得。
  • PC sampler每个noise time先用任意reverse-SDE predictor前进一步，再用score-based MCMC corrector逼近当前pt；本文常用1000 predictor+1000 corrector score evaluations。
  • probability-flow ODE dx=[\(f\)−g²∇log pt/2]dt与forward/reverse SDE具有相同one-time marginals，不是同一随机path law；以sθ代入后成为continuous normalizing flow。
  • log density沿ODE由divergence积分；高维trace用Hutchinson estimator，RK45 \(atol=rtol=10\)⁻5，CIFAR bits/dim对test set做5次估计平均。
  • conditional reverse score加∇\(x\) log pt(\(y\)|\(x\))；class guidance另训time-dependent classifier，inpainting/colorization则利用known coordinates或heuristic likelihood gradients。

Card 06 有效性与局限

有效性与局限

  • 所谓“exact likelihood”指learned probability-flow ODE有标准change-of-variables density，而不是score网络等于真实data score；数值ODE tolerance与Hutchinson trace仍引入误差/随机性。
  • SDE与probability-flow ODE共享marginal distributions，不共享individual stochastic trajectories；不能把deterministic latent path解释为原reverse diffusion某条唯一物理路径。
  • pT通常仅近似forward终点分布，计算避开t=0：VE训练/求解和VP likelihood常用ε=10⁻5，VP sampling \(\epsilon=10\)⁻3；更小ε会改变likelihood与稳定性。
  • PC sampler有SNR、corrector steps、predictor与noise interpolation等hyperparameters；论文逐格搜索CIFAR SNR并承认sampler breadth增加调参负担。
  • Table 3挑全训练过程最低FID，而architecture exploration又用checkpoint区间平均；选择规则不同会带来optimistic selection，不能混合排名。
  • FID基于50k generated samples且所有方法最后加一次Tweedie denoise；论文指出denoising步骤会影响FID，跨论文比较需核对此细节。
  • conditional generation需额外classifier或对measurement score的近似；它不是unconditional score自动精确解任意inverse problem。
  • 作者明确承认sampling仍慢于同数据GAN；高质量通常约1000–2000 score evaluations，2.20并非快速生成结果。
  • 本站未下载checkpoint、采样或重算FID/NLL。

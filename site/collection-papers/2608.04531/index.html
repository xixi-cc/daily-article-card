Discretization and Statistical Consistency of Functional Flow Matching

函数流匹配的离散化与统计一致性

Functional flow matching is posed on distributions of functions but implemented from finitely many coefficients or point values. Under scattered or adaptive refinement, the resulting conditioning sigma-algebras need not be nested, so martingale convergence does not justify the sensor limit. We prove strong L2 convergence of finite conditional velocity targets for every strongly consistent sequence of finite-rank reconstructions, with quantitative bounds for orthogonal projections and a point-sensor extension through a regularity space. For learned flows, coupling directly to a population superposition path yields an end-to-end Wasserstein bound without assuming uniqueness of the population finite-dimensional ODE. Card 02 摘要

摘要

  • 有限实现的target是v_m*(\(t\),\(A_{\mathrm{mX}}_{t}\))=E[\(A_{\mathrm{mU}}_{t}\)|\(A_{\mathrm{mX}}_{t}\)]，一般不等于把continuum target v*投影到grid。
  • 即使adaptive/scattered sensors的sigma-algebras不nested，只要finite-rank reconstructions统一有界并strongly converge to identity，conditional targets仍strong \(L_{2}\) converge。
  • 弱continuity equation只给characteristic paths的superposition measure，不自动给unique population ODE；learned-law bound通过coupling绕开这一点。
  • normalized quadrature neural operator可获得mesh-independent output、parameter与state Lipschitz constants，再配Bernstein给fixed-dimension约O-tilde(\(n^{-1}\)) excess risk。
  • 最终Wasserstein error被拆为endpoint、sensor、operator realization、approximation、target refinement、sample、optimization和ODE solver等可审计项。
Card 03 背景

背景

  • functional flow matching把随机PDE solution、field或function distribution视为Hilbert-space law，但训练实际只见有限coefficients或point values。
  • parameter sharing across grids并不证明conditional regression target、operator norm、ODE flow或generated law共享continuum limit。
  • 若σ(\(A_{\mathrm{mX}}_{t}\))不nested，不能直接用martingale convergence；\(A_{\mathrm{mX}}_{t}\)→X_t也不自动允许交换conditioning。
  • point evaluation在L2不连续，必须进入Sobolev等regularity space并提供stable sensor–reconstruction pair。
Card 04 模型与方法

模型与方法

  • 在separable Hilbert H上取absolutely continuous stochastic interpolationX_t与velocityU_t，population target为conditional expectation E[\(U_{t}\)|\(X_{t}\)]。
  • A_m是uniformly bounded finite-rank reconstruction，pointwise strongly趋于I；finite law \(\mu_{t}^{m}=Law(A_{\mathrm{mX}}_{t})\)。
  • continuum regression Pythagoras identity、weak continuity equation与Hilbert superposition principle建立正确marginals；确定flow map另需Carathéodory/Lipschitz representative。
  • target convergence证明以bounded continuous \(H\)-valued functions逼近V=v*(\(\tau\),Xτ)，再利用g(\(\tau\),A_mXτ)是finite observation可测与conditional projection最优性。
  • operator class用positive quadrature weights和kernel layers；mass-matrix norm equivalence把nodal weighted norm稳定搬到H。
  • learning theorem假设bounded output/target envelopeB、parameter sensitivityG和p-dimensional radiusR；覆盖approximate ERM且不要求convexity或realizability。
Card 05 核心结果与证据

核心结果与证据

  • Theorem 7证明v_m*(\(\tau\),A_mXτ)→v*(\(\tau\),Xτ) in \(L_{2}\)，无需nested observations；orthogonal P_m时误差分成velocity tail与Lipschitz-weighted state tail。
  • Proposition 9用canonical coupling给sup_tW2(\(\mu_{t}^{m}\),\(\mu_{t}\))→0；这是reconstructed population marginal，不需finite ODE uniqueness。
  • Theorem 13构造continuum v*=Bx globally 1-Lipschitz、但finite target Lipschitz constants无统一上界的ℓ2例子；其canonical finite flow却仍精确等于projected continuum flow。
  • Theorem 15在额外mesh-uniform population Lipschitz条件下给L2(\(C\)([0,\(T\)];\(H\))) path convergence；反例说明该条件实质且不能由continuum regularity推出。
  • Theorem 17利用normalized weights与layer magnitude recurrence给quadrature operator的mesh-uniform B、G与state Lipschitz constants，覆盖ReLU、GeLU和SiLU。
  • Theorem 18给high-probability excess risk≤2a_m,\(p\)+1.5ε_opt+O([BGR+B²(\(p\) log \(n\)+log1/\(\delta\))]/\(n\))；realizable case常数更小。
  • Theorem 23只要求learned field稳定，将excess risk转为W2 learned endpoint与finite population endpoint误差≤Te^{Λhat}\(\sqrt risk\)，不要求population target ODE unique。
  • Theorem 26总界为b_end+\(b_{\mathrm{sens}}\)+\(\epsilon_{\mathrm{solver}}\)+Te^{Λhat}\(\sqrt R\)；Table 1继续把approximation分成operator realization、continuum class与conditional-target refinement。
  • noncommuting Gaussian pair在t=1/2投影continuum multiplier为0，但exact conditional finite target为0.72；Table 2以200 repetitions、\(n=128\)–8192回归趋向0.72。
  • clipped exactly-realizable Gaussian scaling例选m,\(n\),\(r\),\(h\) schedule后得到W2=O((log \(n\))/\(\sqrt n\))，并以summable failure probabilities给eventual almost-sure statement。
Card 06 有效性与局限

有效性与局限

  • 定理要求A_m strong consistency和uniform boundedness；arbitrary refinement、unstable interpolation或unseen-frequency recovery不在覆盖范围。
  • point sensors需regularity V嵌入H、same-information sigma algebra和stable reconstruction；对L2 unit ball不存在uniform finite-sensor recovery。
  • target \(L_{2}\) convergence不自动给trajectory convergence；需population mesh-uniform stability，或对learned-law至少需learned field mesh-uniform Lipschitz control。
  • Theorem 13反例使用deterministic coupled transport而非independent Gaussian endpoints；独立端点平滑是否仍造成Lipschitz blowup留作open problem。
  • quadrature operator得到证书，但标准FNO Fourier layers仍需单独mesh-uniform bounds和realization estimate，不能直接套结论。
  • fast statistical rate依bounded/clipped data、fixed or controlledp、B/G/R envelopes和approximate ERM；untruncated Gaussian需moment/localized analysis。
  • explicitGaussian rate使用exactly realizable clipped scaling coupling，不是一般independent-endpoint functional flow matching benchmark。
  • Gaussian Table 2只验证conditioning multiplier，未测试operator approximation、optimization、ODE integration或high-dimensional empirical performance。
  • 控制的是Wasserstein laws而非相对于不存在的infinite-dimensional Lebesgue density或likelihood；likelihood需quasi-invariance machinery。
  • end-to-end bound可能含e^{Λhat}放大，实际usefulness取决于learned stability constant能否小且mesh-independent。

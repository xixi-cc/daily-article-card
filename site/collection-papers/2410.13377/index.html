TITLE · ABSTRACT

Active-hydraulic flows solve the 6-vertex model (and vice versa)

主动水力流求解六顶点模型，反之亦然

By confining colloidal active fluids in microchannel networks, we demonstrate that their degenerate flows correspond to the configurations of the six-vertex model. We use this quantitative correspondence to control and explain the active flows that emerge in square grid networks. In particular, we show that the Lagrangian trajectories of active particles realize the Baxter-Kelland-Wu mapping and form completely packed loops, whose geometry can be exactly predicted and explained. We then go beyond the square-grid geometry and introduce a general framework for predicting the geometry of active-hydraulic flows in arbitrary networks.

Card 02 摘要

摘要

  • 二维Quincke rollers被限制在1,800条等截面square-grid channels；稳态每条edge current集中在±J0且节点守恒，强制two-in/two-out ice rule。
  • 四类turning vertices与两类straight/crossing vertices的出现频率不等，实验测得的wi定义一个非等权six-vertex ensemble。
  • 在junction加入半径r的cylindrical posts连续调w5+\(w_{6}\)，从disordered critical flow推向alternating-circulation ordered flow。
  • tracked particle trajectories在93%的155,000条junction passages上实现Baxter–Kelland–Wu pairing，形成completely packed self-avoiding loops。
  • 已知loop/percolation exponents预测largest-loop 4/7、other loops 3/4、\(P(L)\sim L\)^−5/2、\(C(r)\sim r\)^−4/3，并与实验collapse比较。
Card 03 背景

背景

  • six-vertex模型给square-lattice edges定向，每个四配位vertex恰有两入两出；六种local states的Boltzmann weights决定phase。
  • active fluid在slender channel中自发形成近定速laminar flow；uniform density后的mass conservation把continuous velocity field降为edge signs。
  • 早期spin-ice假设六vertex等权，但实际junction geometry偏好特定流型；必须测weights才能从local constraints预测macroscopic morphology。
  • ‘双向’含义是active hydraulics给出vertex configurations，而integrable-model知识反过来预测flow/trajectory statistics；不是microscopic Navier–Stokes与lattice Hamiltonian逐自由度等价。
Card 04 模型与方法

模型与方法

  • edge law \(J\)〈i,j〉=±J0与node law ΣjJ〈i,j〉=0在四配位网络生成六种allowed vertices；实验ρ0≈8,500 mm^−2，\(J_{0}\approx 1\),300 s^−1。
  • 无post时十次重复给w1–\(4=0.20\pm 0.01\)、\(w_{5}\)–\(6=0.09\pm 0.01\)；vertex reversal与axisymmetry使成对权重相等。
  • 加入0–70 μm circular posts改变junction stagnation geometry；测vertex counts与plaquette circulation order parameter \(C\)=〈|Ci|〉。
  • six-vertex configuration在每个vertex按两种pairing拆成two-colored loops；supplement用complex phases把Z6V精确重写为loop fugacity \(N\)=2cosγ的CPL partition function。
  • 实验trajectory tracking确定实际pairing，避免仅从edge arrows随意连接；统计loop length、gyration radius和same-loop two-point probability。
  • 补充材料再用CPL↔Potts random-cluster/bond-percolation映射，调用critical percolation hull与self-avoiding polymer exponents。
  • 十次independent realizations用于disordered statistics；不同post radii用于检查universality collapse。
Card 05 核心结果与证据

核心结果与证据

  • Figure 1显示density窄分布与current双峰，支持把edge coarse-grain成±\(J_{0}\)；六种node velocity fields与six vertices一一对应。
  • Figure 2b中w5+w6随post radius从约0.\(18\pm 0\).03升到1；在w5,6约为w1–4一半的theoretical boundary附近circulation上升并形成alternate plaquette order。
  • 正文一处称antiferroelectric、Figure 2 caption称ferro-electric phase；从flow图看order parameter是交替circulation，卡片以该可观测描述避免标签歧义。
  • Figure 3b报告93%的155,000 tracked trajectories遵守BKW pairing；剩余7%提示mapping是高精度实验规律而非每次passage绝对constraint。
  • largest system-spanning loop的内部距离尺度按ν=4/7，其他loops的Rg∼\(L^{3}\)/4；Figure 3c分开画两类，不能混成单一fractal exponent。
  • loop-length distribution与same-loop correlation分别吻合−5/2和−4/3；三个小post radii、十次实验collapse支持disordered flows落在同一critical universality class。
  • ordered极限主要由vertices 5/6组成，流绕单plaquette形成length-4 loops；domain walls会带额外vortices，纯six-vertex bulk描述需扩展。
  • 实验‘solve six-vertex’指物理产生符合测得weights的configurations并实现几何统计；没有报告mixing time、independent Boltzmann sampling检验或利用装置计算partition function。
Card 06 有效性与局限

有效性与局限

  • one-to-one arrow mapping依赖steady homogeneous density、equal channel cross sections与constant-magnitude currents；transient/compressible/heterogeneous networks可违反±J0简化。
  • vertex frequencies足以定义independent six-vertex ensemble的主张依赖nonzero-current edges上local deformation在channel内relax，邻vertex耦合很弱；未给完整higher-order mutual information审计。
  • \(J=0\) edges承载subchannel vortices并耦合邻junction handedness，需要interaction rules或19-vertex扩展；一般网络不再是独立six-vertex。
  • post tuning同时改变weights和局部流体结构；phase-boundary comparison样本点有限，未做critical exponent/finite-size scaling of circulation。
  • 93% trajectory compliance不是100%；tracking errors、junction ambiguity或真实non-BKW events对tail loop statistics可能有系统影响。
  • exponent predictions适用于critical disordered regime与给定boundary/loop fugacity；finite grid、largest-loop selection和domain walls会产生finite-size corrections。
  • 装置采样的configuration probability是否严格∏wi/Z未由detailed balance或transition dynamics证明；active system本身耗散且不必满足equilibrium kinetics。
  • 只测试二维square grid与圆post控制；arbitrary topology的四条规则是proposal，三维复杂network尚被作者明确列为挑战。
  • weights与interaction rules必须实验标定，故框架减少subchannel simulation但不是完全无参数预测。
  • 本站未重析trajectory数据或独立验证partition-function mapping。
Card 07 复现与资源

复现与资源

  • 复现实验先验证每edge ρ和J分布、stationarity与node residual；预注册将edge分为−\(J_{0}\)/0/+J0的阈值及uncertain cases。
  • 对每个post radius多次独立turn-on，报告six vertex counts、spatial correlations、circulation、domain walls与finite-grid position dependence。
  • 检验product-weight ensemble不能只匹配marginals：采样six-vertex Monte Carlo并比较structure factor、loop statistics、vertex-pair correlations和configuration likelihood。
  • trajectory层保留raw tracks、junction pairing与ambiguous labels；报告93%分子分母、tracking quality和对loop exponents的sensitivity。
  • 拟合ν、\(\tau\)、2x2时明确scale window与finite-size correction，largest loop和ordinary loops分开；bootstrap按experiment而非按trajectory避免伪重复。
  • 推广odd coordination或J=0 networks时显式加入zero-flow vertices与interaction costs，再用held-out geometry检验，而不是仅用同一square grid校准。

TITLE · ABSTRACT

Algorithmic threshold for high-dimensional projection pursuit I: general theory

高维投影追寻的算法阈值 I：一般理论

For high-dimensional Gaussian data, the paper characterizes which one-dimensional projection laws can be attained by dimension-free Lipschitz algorithms on spherical or Ising domains. A one-dimensional stochastic control problem gives matching algorithmic thresholds, with hardness from a branching overlap-gap argument and attainability through Brownian-bridge incremental AMP.

TITLE · ABSTRACT

Bayesian Tracking of a Diffusing Target in Two and Three Dimensions

二维与三维扩散目标的贝叶斯追踪

A noisy sensor array tracking a diffusing target has two distinct failure modes in three dimensions: diffuse uncertainty (EW) and a sharp but wrong posterior peak (KPZ). Bayes-optimal multicriticality is supported by symmetry, polymer results and finite-size scans; infinite-order depinning remains conjectural.

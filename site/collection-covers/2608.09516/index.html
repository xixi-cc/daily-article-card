HarnessWAM: Bridging Prediction and Deliberation in World Action Models

HarnessWAM：弥合世界动作模型的预测与审议鸿沟

World Action Models provide finite-horizon prediction and local control but do not by themselves maintain global task state, verify outcomes, or recover across long embodied episodes. HarnessWAM adds a model-external VLM Task Manager with evidence-grounded scene belief and a structured task graph, projects open-ended plans into the validated skill interface of the WAM, and coordinates execution through a fast progress loop and event-triggered slow deliberation. With a fixed LingBot-VA checkpoint in controlled comparisons, the framework reaches 59.6% full-task and 69.9% subtask success on RoboMemArena; it reaches 23.7% on RoboCerebra Ideal. Ablations identify executable-space projection as the largest contributor, while task state, progress events, semantic verification, and local embodiment recovery make separable contributions.
